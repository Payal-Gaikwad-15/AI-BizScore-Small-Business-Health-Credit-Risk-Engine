## 🚨 The Problem

Small businesses can struggle to obtain credit because traditional evaluation may focus heavily on the owner's credit score or limited financial information.

But a business can have a good personal credit score and still be financially unhealthy because of:

- 💸 High operating costs
- 💳 Excessive debt
- 📉 High customer churn
- 📑 Overdue invoices
- 💰 Low profit margins

So the real question is:

> **"Is this business financially healthy enough to handle more credit?"**

Traditional evaluation may not provide a complete picture of the business's actual financial and operational health.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AI BizScore evaluates the business as a whole using Machine Learning.**

The user provides:

💰 Monthly Revenue  
💸 Monthly Operating Costs  
💳 Total Business Debt  
📉 Customer Churn Rate  
👤 Owner Credit Score  
📑 Overdue Pending Invoices

🎯 **Business Health Score**  
🚦 **Financial Risk Rating**  
📊 **Financial KPIs**  
🔍 **Risk Drivers**  
📝 **Actionable Recommendations**  
🧪 **What-If Growth Simulation**

## 🗂️ Dataset

AI BizScore was developed using a **40,000-record synthetic business dataset** designed to represent different financial and operational conditions of small businesses.

### 📊 Dataset Shape

- 📌 **Total Records:** 40,000
- 🧩 **Total Columns:** 7
- 🔢 **Input Features:** 6
- 🎯 **Target Variable:** `default_risk`

### 📋 Dataset Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 💰 **Monthly Revenue** | Total revenue generated by the business each month |
| 💸 **Monthly Operating Costs** | Total expenses required to operate the business each month |
| 💳 **Total Business Debt** | Total outstanding debt or loans held by the business |
| 📉 **Customer Churn Rate** | Percentage of customers who stop doing business with the company |
| 👤 **Owner Credit Score** | Creditworthiness score of the business owner |
| 📑 **Overdue Pending Invoices** | Number of invoices that remain unpaid after their due date |
| 🎯 **default_risk** | Target variable indicating the business risk category |

### ⚖️ Risk Class Distribution

🔴 **High Risk Class (1): 93.3%**  
🟢 **Low/No Risk Class (0): 6.7%**

> 📌 The dataset is highly imbalanced. Therefore, model performance is evaluated using **precision, recall, F1-score, and cross-validation**, rather than relying only on accuracy.

---

## 🤖 Machine Learning Model

**XGBoost Classifier** is used to learn patterns between the financial/operational features and the `default_risk` target.

### 🔄 5-Fold Cross-Validation

**Fold Accuracy Scores:**

`97.81% • 97.99% • 98.05% • 97.78% • 97.74%`

🎯 **Average Cross-Validation Accuracy: 97.87%**

### 📈 Test Set Performance

The model achieved **98% accuracy on 8,000 test records**.

| Metric | Class 0 | Class 1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 🎯 Precision | 92% | 98% |
| 🔍 Recall | 76% | 100% |
| ⚖️ F1-Score | 83% | 99% |

**Overall Accuracy:** 98%  
**Macro F1-Score:** 91%  
**Weighted F1-Score:** 98%

## 📊 Business Health Score

The application converts the business condition into a simple **5–98 Business Health Score**:

🟢 **PRIME — 70+**  
🟡 **MODERATE — 45–69**  
🔴 **HIGH RISK — Below 45**

The dashboard also calculates:

💰 **Monthly Profit**  
📈 **Profit Margin**  
💳 **Debt-to-Revenue Ratio**

## 🔍 Explainable AI

AI BizScore doesn't just provide a prediction.

It uses **feature-importance analysis** to identify which business factors have the strongest influence on the model's evaluation.

This makes the result easier for a **non-technical business owner** to understand.

Instead of simply saying:

> ❌ "Your business is risky."

The system helps answer:

> 🔍 **"What factors are contributing to the risk?"**
